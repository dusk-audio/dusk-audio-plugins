# Third-party notices — Chord Analyzer Headless

The native headless LV2 uses the LV2 SDK's public core, atom, MIDI and URID
headers (ISC). Its binary links the shared Dusk Audio numeric core; it includes
no JUCE, DAF UI, fonts or graphics backend.
