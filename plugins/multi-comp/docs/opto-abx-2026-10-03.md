# Opto: blind ABX against the UAD LA-2A (2026-10-02/03)

Owner question (2026-10-02 evening): two weeks of instrument-driven parity rounds produced no actionable
result. What does a "cannot hear the difference" release gate say about the current OPTO mode?

## Method

- Device under test: the installed MC-2 AU (`multi-comp-2.component`, R11a physical base), rendered offline
  through `opto-host-20260930/duskverb_render` with `Mode=0`, `Gain=0.25`, `Peak Reduction` and `Limit Mode`
  set per trial. Readbacks match the UAD captures (PR 50 -> "50", Gain .25 -> "22"); the PR-0 linear gain is
  1.123 on both devices.
- Reference: the 2026-09-23 native UAD captures of the real-music corpus (30 clips of 10 s, FIT/VAL/SEALED).
- Pairs aligned (UAD latency 87 samples, MC-2 73), RMS level-matched per trial, 10 ms fades, -1 dBFS ceiling,
  24-bit 48 kHz. A/B assignment and X drawn from a seeded RNG; the key was not read before scoring.
- Builder and scorer: tools repo `plugins/MultiComp/tests/programme_parity/opto/abx_kit.py`.
  Evidence: `build-multi-comp-1176/opto-abx-20261002/` (local).

## Round 1: 16 trials, 14 pairs + 2 null controls (UAD vs itself)

Settings: Comp .5, Comp .625, Comp 1, Limit .8125. Clips chosen from the worst instrument rows (bass DI,
kick, drum bus, bass amp in Limit) plus vocals, piano, mixes and the owner's own songs.

| | Result |
|---|---|
| Pairs | 8 / 14 correct, one-sided binomial p = 0.395 |
| Pairs rated "probably" | 4 / 7 |
| Null controls | both rated "probably" (a difference heard where none exists) |
| Only "sure" answer | trial 9, f_kick Comp .625, correct |

The three clips the instrument flags worst (bass DI 2.3 dB rms, mix, mysongmix) were answered wrong with
"probably".

## Round 2: kick only, 8 fresh pairs + 1 null (seed 20261003)

5 / 8 correct, p = 0.363. Null control answered wrong. Seven of nine answers were "B".

Combined over both rounds: 13 / 22 pairs, p = 0.262.

## Verdict

At chance in both rounds. The owner cannot distinguish MC-2 OPTO from the UAD LA-2A on this corpus at these
settings after level matching. The grey-box R1 fit, which reached DEV 0.406 dB rms at iteration 6 against the
0.455 baseline, is an improvement of 0.05 dB on a quantity already below the hearing threshold measured here.

Static level offsets at identical knob positions remain measurable: MC-2 is 0.5-0.7 dB louder than UAD on
dense programme at Comp .5 (less gain reduction) and 0.96 dB quieter on bass amp at Limit .8125 (more). A user
hears that as a Gain-knob calibration difference, not as character.

Limits of the test: 14 + 8 trials detect only a difference heard most of the time; rare or material-specific
differences outside the 30 clips are not excluded. Level matching removes the static offsets above by design.

## Recommendation

Ship OPTO as it stands. Close the parity campaign: stop the grey-box fit, keep the CTest regression bounds
exactly where they are (they guard the shipped behaviour, not parity), keep the native captures for a future
re-ABX if the cell is ever changed. If a user report ever names a specific setting and material, re-run
`abx_kit.py focus` on it before touching the DSP.
